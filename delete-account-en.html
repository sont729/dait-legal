Delete your Harulook account and data

In the app

  1. Open Harulook and go to Account.
  2. Select Delete account.
  3. Read the notice and confirm deletion.

If you cannot use the app

Email iter729@gmail.com from the address connected to your Harulook account. We may ask for limited information to verify ownership before deletion.

Correction and advance notice: retained transaction records

Correction and notice date: October 26, 2026 · Planned effective date: November 3, 2026

The previous Privacy Policy statement that billing records were retained only until one day after the billing period ended did not match the actual payment and credit ledger. Minimum records for purchase verification, refunds, credit restoration and duplicate-grant prevention remain after account deletion. Photos and inquiry text are not retained in this ledger.

The planned rules retain necessary contract, payment and service-delivery records for five years after the transaction ends, protect records needed for valid credits or pending refunds and disputes, and remove unnecessary records after retention expires. Existing records without verifiable end-date evidence use November 3, 2026 as the starting point. Support photos remain subject to 30 days and inquiry text to 90 days. Expiry cleanup will not begin before both November 3 and seven days after advance notice through an in-app notice or push notification; this is not a statement that the new automated deletion is already complete. See the Privacy Policy correction and planned rules for calculation, protected rights and separate consumer-dispute records.

What is deleted

Your account, profile, saved looks, reactions, recommendation history, and other account-linked app data are deleted or anonymized as described in the Privacy Policy. Minimum payment and credit-processing records remain separately as explained in the correction and planned retention rules above. Coordinate-free location-use records required by law are retained for up to six months. Support inquiries and review photos are also scheduled for deletion when the account is deleted. Deleting your Harulook account does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription; cancel it in your store account first.
