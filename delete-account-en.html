Delete your Dait account and data

In the app

  1. Open Dait and go to Account.
  2. Select Delete account.
  3. Read the notice and confirm deletion.

If you cannot use the app

Email iter729@gmail.com from the address connected to your Dait account. We may ask for limited information to verify ownership before deletion.

What is deleted

Your account, profile, saved looks, reactions, recommendation history, and other account-linked app data are deleted or anonymized as described in the Privacy Policy. Records that must be retained for fraud prevention, payment, tax, or legal compliance are kept only for the required period. Deleting your Dait account does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription; cancel it in your store account first.
