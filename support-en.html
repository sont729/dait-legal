Using Dait

  • If weather does not refresh, check location permission and your network connection, then refresh in the app.
  • Outfit reminders require notifications to be enabled in both Dait and iPhone Settings.
  • To add a Home Screen widget, touch and hold the Home Screen and choose Dait from the widget gallery.
  • Apple Watch features are available after Dait is installed on a paired Watch.

Dait Plus

Restore or manage a purchase from My Account → DAIT PLUS in the iPhone app.

You can also manage or cancel auto-renewal in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Deleting a Dait account does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription. Check auto-renewal in subscription management before deleting your account.

Account and privacy

You can request account deletion from My Account → Delete all my data in the app.
