Location Terms

Dait · Effective August 15, 2026

1. Purpose

These Terms describe how Dait uses location information to provide local weather and outfit guidance.

2. Location-based features and fees

  • Retrieve weather for your current latitude and longitude.
  • Recommend outfits and preparation items for local conditions.
  • Check weather changes for reminders you enable.

Dait does not charge a separate fee for location features. Your network provider may charge data fees.

3. Data used and retention

Your current coordinates are sent over HTTPS through Dait's Cloudflare proxy to WeatherAPI for weather retrieval. Dait does not store exact coordinates in its database or shared proxy cache. If you enable a location-based reminder, the last location needed for that reminder may be stored locally on your device until you disable the reminder, clear app data, or uninstall the app.

For Korean legal compliance, Dait may retain a coordinate-free record showing that a location feature was used, including an anonymous identifier, time, purpose, acquisition method, and processing method, for six months before deletion.

4. International processing

Cloudflare, Inc. and WeatherAPI.com (Zoomash Ltd) process coordinates only as needed to route and answer a weather request. Processing occurs when you request weather or when an enabled reminder checks weather. Their processing is also governed by their service and privacy terms.

5. Your choices

  • Grant, deny, or withdraw device location permission at any time.
  • Use a manually selected location instead of current location.
  • Disable location-based reminders and delete local app data.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of applicable records by contacting us.

Withdrawing permission stops current-location features but does not prevent you from choosing a location manually.

6. Accuracy and availability

Location and weather may be delayed, approximate, or unavailable because of device settings, network conditions, third-party services, or maintenance. Do not rely on Dait as the only source for emergency or safety decisions.
