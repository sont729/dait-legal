You can view your own inquiry; review is performed by Harulook support staff with separately assigned access. Photo-viewing links last no more than five minutes and never extend beyond the photo retention deadline. This support process does not send photos or inquiries back to a generative AI provider or use them for advertising or model training.   • Photo and inquiry retention: Photos are scheduled for deletion 30 days after inquiry creation; descriptions, conversations and processing history after 90 days.   • Minimal credit-processing records: After inquiry or account deletion, credit-transaction links, hashes of the generation, reviewer and decision note, and processing times remain with the existing credit ledger to prevent duplicate restoration and handle reversals of the original transaction. This ledger contains no photos or inquiry text. Hashing is not treated as making all such information anonymous.

4 Data Retention and Deletion

Retention Period

We retain personal information for the duration of your use of the App. Locally stored data (AsyncStorage) is removed when the App is deleted. Server data for guests is keyed to an anonymous identifier (UUID). If you choose to sign in, the email address, display name, and account identifier supplied by your provider are linked to your Supabase Auth account. You may delete the account and related data through Delete All My Data or by email request, and we will destroy the data without undue delay. Error diagnostics are limited to five records per account per day and deleted within 30 days. Location-use confirmation records required by Article 16(2) of Korea's Location Information Act exclude coordinates and are retained for six months before deletion.

Deletion Methods

  • Electronic files are permanently deleted using methods that prevent recovery.
  • Exact coordinates are not stored in Harulook's database or relay cache. They are stored on device only when weather alerts are enabled and are removed when alerts are disabled, through in-app data deletion, or when the App is deleted.
  • Location-use confirmation records contain no coordinates and are deleted after six months.
  • Calendar data is used only to display schedules and support on-device outfit suggestions, and is not stored on our servers.
  • Harulook Wallpapers and Harulook watch photos are stored in the on-device photo library and are never deleted automatically by the App. You can revoke Photos access or delete the images or albums directly.
  • Fashion try-on: Harulook handles input photos in server memory during generation and does not retain them as part of that process. Separately submitted support copies follow the additional support notice above. Server results are blocked after 7 days and removed by a cleanup job. Results saved in the App or Photos stay on the device until you delete them. Usage and billing records, kept apart from photos, are retained until one day after the relevant billing period ends.
  • Outfit weather coach: Harulook does not store the original photo, and analyzed clothing attributes are deleted after 7 days. Deleting your account also deletes related records.
  • Wardrobe garment analysis: Harulook does not store the original photo on its servers. Server analysis caches and request records expire or become eligible for deletion after 30 days; expired caches are not reused. Cleanup runs on an hourly schedule and during subsequent requests, so actual deletion may be delayed by scheduler availability and server responses. On-device wardrobe photos, cutouts and garment details remain until you delete them. Account deletion also marks on-device wardrobe items and server analysis records for deletion.
  • Meta Muse fashion try-on uses Standard Services. Meta states that it does not use this content to train Meta models, but may process and retain it to provide services, comply with law, and review safety, security, abuse and policy violations. Provider retention is separate from Harulook's input and 7-day result policies. See Meta's data commitments and service terms.
  • Processing and retention by Google for the outfit weather coach follow Google's terms. The paid Gemini API states that inputs and outputs are not used to improve models, but they may be logged for a limited period for safety and abuse detection.
  • Wardrobe garment analysis requests via OpenRouter are sent with data collection and model training disabled (`data_collection: deny`). Safety, abuse-prevention logging, and retention by OpenRouter and its inference infrastructure follow the OpenRouter Privacy Policy.

9 Data Security and On-Device Photo Retouching

We implement the following measures to ensure the security of your personal information:

  • HTTPS (TLS) encryption for all data transmissions
  • Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) policies for data access control
  • Fashion try-on results are delivered only to their owner from private file storage using expiring signed URLs
  • On-device photo retouching: Face and body retouching analyzes facial landmarks, contours and pose on your device to adjust the image. Harulook uses these measurements for photo editing, not to recognize your identity or authenticate an account. Separately requested cloud AI features send selected photos to the providers in Sections 6 and 7; provider processing and retention are separate from on-device retouching
  • Guest access through anonymous Supabase Auth UUIDs, with only the Google or Apple account information selected by the user collected for optional sign-in
  • Exact coordinates are not stored on servers and are discarded immediately after weather retrieval
  • Coordinate-free location-use confirmation records are automatically recorded and deleted after six months
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability monitoring

10 Cookies, Tracking, and No Sale or Sharing of Personal Information

The App does not use web cookies. However, we may collect in-app usage data (views, likes, saves) for service improvement. You can opt out through the in-app data deletion feature or by uninstalling the App.

No Sale or Sharing (CCPA/CPRA): The App does not collect or use behavioral information for personalized advertising and does not "sell" personal information or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. It is configured to serve only non-personalized ads through Google AdMob. During ad delivery, advertising identifiers and device information may be processed for ad serving, frequency capping, and fraud prevention. You can limit or reset the advertising identifier in your device settings (iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; Android: Settings › Google › Ads). Choosing not to watch rewarded ads does not restrict basic service use; only the additional outfit feature may be unavailable.

11 Users Under 18

The App is intended for users aged 18 or older and asks users to confirm eligibility during onboarding. If we confirm use by a person under 18, we stop their service access and delete unnecessary personal information without undue delay, except minimal records we are legally required to retain.
